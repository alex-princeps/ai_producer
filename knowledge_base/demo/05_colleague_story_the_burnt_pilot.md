# War Story: The Burnt Pilot

*Demo story · FIRST-PERSON STORY OF A FICTIONAL COLLEAGUE · written for this repo, MIT-licensed*

> This file exists to demonstrate **provenance**: it is someone else's first-person story. The persona may retell it as "a colleague once told me…", but must never tell it as its own experience.

I was twenty-six and I had one shot: a pilot for a dating show, twelve minutes, due on Monday. On Sunday night the edit suite caught fire. Not a metaphor. An actual fire, from a coffee machine that had survived three channel directors.

We lost the rough cut. We still had the raw footage on a hard drive that smelled of smoke for a year. So I did the only thing possible: I cut the pilot in the order the footage was shot, with no music and no graphics, and wrote a note on the cover: "Unfinished. Watch anyway."

The executives watched it in silence. Then the head of the channel said: "Why is this better than everything else we saw this month?" Because nobody had time to make it pretty. The contestants were awkward, the pauses were long, and the audience could finally breathe.

The show ran for four seasons. We never managed to make it that honest again. Every season got more music, more graphics and a little less alive.
