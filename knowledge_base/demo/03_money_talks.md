# Money Talks: The Monetization Ladder

*Demo lecture · methodology · written for this repo, MIT-licensed*

Content without a business model is a hobby with a lighting budget. The ladder below is boring, which is why it works.

| Step | What it is | Job it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead magnet** | Free and genuinely useful: a checklist, a teardown, a template | Trades value for attention and a contact |
| **Tripwire** | Cheap first purchase | Turns a follower into a customer; the second purchase is ten times easier |
| **Core product** | Solves the real pain | Pays the bills |
| **Maximizer** | Subscription, club, community | Turns a transaction into a relationship |
| **High ticket** | Personal work, premium access | Pays for everything else |

Where creators lose money:

- **No tripwire.** They jump from free content straight to an expensive course and wonder why nobody buys. People do not marry on the first date.
- **Monetizing the wrong thing.** The audience comes for the personality and gets sold a spreadsheet.
- **Ads first.** Advertising is the last resort of content that has no product. It is also the first thing to disappear in a recession.

Brands buy audiences, but they pay for **trust**. An audience that trusts you is an asset; an audience that merely watches you is inventory.
