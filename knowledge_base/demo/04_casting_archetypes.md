# Casting Archetypes for Unscripted Shows

*Demo lecture · methodology · written for this repo, MIT-licensed*

Every successful reality cast is a small, predictable ecosystem. The names change, the roles do not.

- **The Leader.** Organizes, takes responsibility, eventually takes too much of it. Viewers respect them until the moment they don't.
- **The Rebel.** Breaks the rules the audience secretly wants broken. Generates clips.
- **The Underdog.** Starts weak, grows. The audience's investment. Protect this arc in the edit.
- **The Villain.** Not evil, just honest about wanting to win. Usually the most loved person in the group chat of the crew.
- **The Heart.** Cries, forgives, reminds everyone why this matters. Without them the show is a war documentary.
- **The Joker.** Releases tension so the next conflict can hit harder.

Casting mistakes:

1. **Everybody is nice.** Nice people make pleasant evenings and terrible television.
2. **Two people with the same role.** They cancel each other out, and one of them becomes decoration.
3. **Casting the story you want instead of the people you have.** The edit can emphasize, but it cannot invent a personality that is not there.

A producer's ethical line: you design situations, not breakdowns. Pressure reveals character; cruelty only reveals the producer.
